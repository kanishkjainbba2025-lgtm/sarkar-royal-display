# Sarkar Regal Showcase

Build a premium, high-converting e-commerce product page for a luxury perfume brand called "Sarkar". The specific product is "Sarkar Regal". The UI should be modern, minimalist, and mobile-responsive, replicating a high-end luxury Shopify storefront.

**Color Palette & Typography:**

*   **Background:** Clean white and soft light gray for maximum contrast.

*   **Accents:** Royal deep purple, burgundy, matte black, and subtle gold accents to represent the "Regal" luxury theme. 

*   **Typography:** Elegant sans-serif for headers (like Montserrat or Playfair Display for a luxurious touch) and clean sans-serif for body text (like Inter or Roboto).

**Header / Navigation:**

*   **Logo:** "Sarkar" in bold, minimalist, high-end text on the top left.

*   **Center Links:** Men Collection, Women Collection, New Arrivals, Best Sellers, Offers.

*   **Right Icons:** Minimalist icons for Search, User Profile/Account, and Shopping Cart (with a small notification dot).

**Main Hero Section (Split Layout: 50% Images / 50% Details):**

**Left Column (Image Gallery):**

*   Create a vertical thumbnail gallery on the far left, with a large main product image in the center.

*   **Image Generation Prompt for placeholders:** A luxurious, heavy 100ml glass perfume bottle shaped like a King chess piece with a regal crown cap. The glass is a rich, transparent deep purple/burgundy. Include secondary images showcasing the packaging: a premium matte black/purple cylindrical box and a sleek rectangular outer box with gold typography.

**Right Column (Product Details - Top to Bottom):**

*   **Title:** "Sarkar Regal Parfum (100ml)" in a large, elegant font.

*   **Reviews:** 5 gold stars followed by "(420+ Reviews)" in subtle gray text.

*   **Pricing:** "₹1,199" in large, bold text. Right next to it, include a strikethrough MRP of "₹1,999" and a highlighted badge saying "Save 40%". Include "Inclusive of all taxes" in small text underneath.

*   **Short Description:** "The Ultimate Party & Date Night Fragrance. A sweet, warm, and seductive Oud & Amber Parfum designed to make a statement. Excellent 10-12 hour longevity."

*   **Fragrance Profile / Badges:** Add small, elegant icons with text: "Sweet Oud", "Amber", "Long Lasting".

*   **Quantity Selector:** A clean [+] and [-] box.

*   **Call to Action Buttons:** 

    *   Primary Button: A full-width, bold black button with white text saying "Add to Cart".

    *   Secondary Button: A full-width gold or deep purple button saying "Buy it Now".

*   **Trust Badges:** Small icons below the buttons indicating "Free Shipping", "100% Authentic", and "Secure Checkout".

**Accordion / Expandable Tabs (Below the Buy buttons):**

*   **Tab 1: Fragrance Notes (Open by default):** "Top Notes: Bergamot, Cinnamon, Apple. Heart: Sweet Woods. Base: Smoky Oud, Amber, Vanilla."

*   **Tab 2: Longevity & Performance:** "On Clothes: 10 to 12 hours. On Skin: 7 to 9 hours. Strong projection for the first 3-4 hours."

*   **Tab 3: Shipping & Returns:** "Free express shipping across India. Hassle-free 7-day returns on unopened boxes."

**Footer:**

*   A clean, dark matte footer. Include a Newsletter signup box ("Subscribe for exclusive offers"), links to Contact Us, Refund Policy, Privacy Policy, and minimalist social media icons (Instagram, WhatsApp, Facebook).
